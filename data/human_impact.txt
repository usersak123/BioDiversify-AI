HUMAN IMPACT

Human activities affecting biodiversity include land conversion, habitat destruction, deforestation, pollution, pesticide use and intensive resource extraction.

Broad-spectrum pesticide use can affect non-target organisms, depending on the chemical, dose, application method and exposure.

Habitat destruction reduces available resources and ecological niches.

Deforestation can reduce habitat area and increase fragmentation.

Pollution can affect organisms directly or alter ecosystem processes.

Reducing unnecessary chemical inputs, maintaining habitat patches and improving landscape diversity can reduce some pressures on biodiversity.

Human pressures frequently interact rather than acting independently. For example, habitat loss combined with climate stress can increase ecological vulnerability.
